## 2. Data we collect

| Type of data | What it includes | How we get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | Email address, name and profile photo | You give it to us when you sign up, or we receive it from Google if you sign in with Google |
| **Profile and preferences** | Bio, working hours and app settings | You enter them in Focusly |
| **Your content** | Tasks, subtasks, notes, links, tags, projects, documents, time logs and focus sessions | You create it in Focusly or it comes from your Google Calendar |
| **Google Calendar** (if you connect it) | Events in your primary calendar: title, description, dates, attendees and meeting links; also the access tokens Google gives us | From Google, with your authorization |
| **AI assistant** | Your messages and the assistant's answers, plus "memories": information about you that the assistant extracts from your conversations, such as your scheduling preferences | When you use the assistant |
| **Technical data** | IP address, browser type, date and time of requests, and error logs (which may include your email). If you turn on notifications, also your device's notification identifier | Automatically when you use Focusly |

**Other people's data.** If your calendar events include other people, we process their names and email addresses only to show them on the related task.

**Sensitive data.** We do not ask for sensitive data, such as health information, religious beliefs, sexual orientation or ethnic origin. We recommend that you do not include it in your tasks, notes or conversations. If you do, we will process it only to provide the service to you.

## 3. What we use your data for

**Purposes necessary to provide the service:**

- creating and managing your account, and letting you sign in (including emailing you access links);
- storing, displaying and organizing your content;
- syncing your tasks with Google Calendar, if you connect it;
- generating plans, schedules, statistics and productivity insights from your tasks and focus sessions;
- answering your messages through the AI assistant and personalizing its answers with your memories;
- sending you service notices, such as reminders, security alerts or changes to this Notice or the Terms;
- keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fixing errors;
- complying with our legal obligations.

**Secondary purposes (not necessary for the service):**

- sending you Focusly news and promotions;
- analyzing aggregated use of Focusly to improve it.

You can object to secondary purposes at any time by emailing [[CORREO DE CONTACTO]]. Doing so does not affect your use of Focusly.

**We do not sell your personal data** or use it to show you personalized advertising.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

- When you use the assistant, we send our AI provider your message, the history of that conversation and the account information needed to answer. This includes, for example, your tasks, calendar events, working hours, statistics and memories.
- Our AI provider is currently **Google (Gemini models)**. We also use Google to turn your memories into numerical representations ("embeddings") that let us find the most relevant ones for each conversation.
- The provider processes this information to generate the answer, under its data use terms for API customers.
- **We do not use your information, including your Google data, to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.**
- You can delete your conversations from the assistant and ask us to delete your memories by emailing [[CORREO DE CONTACTO]].

## 5. Data we receive from Google

**What we get:**

- If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo.
- If you connect Google Calendar: access to read, create, modify and delete events in your primary calendar.

**How we use it:** only to provide the Focusly features that depend on it. That is, signing in, syncing your tasks with your calendar and giving the AI assistant context when you use it.

**What we don't do with it:**

- we do not sell it or use it for advertising;
- we do not transfer it to third parties, except to the providers needed to provide the service, for security reasons or when the law requires us to;
- no person reads it, except with your express consent, to investigate a security or abuse issue, when the law requires it, or in aggregated and anonymized form for internal operations;
- we do not use it to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

Focusly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke Focusly's access to your Google account at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## 8. Cookies and local storage

- **Essential cookies:** we use the `access_token` and `refresh_token` cookies to keep you signed in. They cannot be accessed from JavaScript, and you cannot sign in without them.
- **Browser local storage:** we store your interface preferences, focus mode state and the email you used to request your access link.
- **We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools.**

You can delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings. If you do, you will be signed out.

## 9. How long we keep your data

- We keep your data while you have an active account.
- When you delete a task, document, project or conversation, it is immediately deleted from our database.
- If you delete your account, we immediately delete your data from our database and your profile photos, and revoke Focusly's access to your Google account. Your Google Calendar events are not deleted.
- Deleted data may remain in backups for up to [[X]] days, after which it is permanently erased.
- Technical logs are kept for up to [[X]] days.
- We may keep certain data longer if the law requires us to.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data and know how we use it;
- **Rectify** it if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **Cancel** it, that is, ask us to delete it;
- **Object** to our using it for certain purposes;
- **Withdraw your consent** and **limit** the use or disclosure of your data.

You can correct much of your data yourself in Focusly, and you can delete your account from **Settings → Security → Delete Account**.

To exercise any other right, email [[CORREO DE CONTACTO]] from the email address linked to your account and include:

- your name;
- the right you want to exercise;
- the data your request refers to.

We will reply within 20 business days at most. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the competent personal data protection authority.

## 11. Security

We protect your data with reasonable technical and organizational measures:

- encrypted connections (HTTPS);
- protected session cookies;
- access links that expire after 15 minutes;
- restricted access to our systems.

No system is completely secure. If an incident significantly affects your rights, we will notify you as required by law.

## 12. Minors
